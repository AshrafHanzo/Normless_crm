The journey of a lead

Every lead follows these steps. Steps marked Bot and CRM happen on their own — you pick up from step 3.

  1. 1

    The customer writes

    Customer

    From a Facebook or Instagram ad (“Came in from an ad” shows on the chat), or straight to +91 73387 23696.

  2. 2

    The menu takes the enquiry

    Bot
    • Product (with its photo, colours and prices) → colour → how many pieces → another product?
    • Printing or embroidery → the logo → a summary with the price per piece.
    • On the way it sends the catalogue PDF, size charts, photos of our work and the price list when asked, and answers common questions (MOQ, sample piece, payment terms, address).

    Sets the status by itself: Qualified once they share details · Important when it’s more than 50 pieces · Need mockup when product, colour, quantity and logo are all in.

  3. 3

    You take the chat from “Reply now”

    You
    • Open the chat and reply — it becomes yours. Read the Lead tab first: products, colours, quantities and the logo are already filled in.
    • Call if they asked, or if the order is big. Send Samples, the catalogue or a quote from the tools under the chat.
    • Fill in anything missing on the Lead tab: exact quantity per product, colour, billing name and GST.
  4. 4

    The designer makes the mockup

    Designer

    Leads at Need mockup go to the design team. The designer uploads the mockup in the chat (Lead tab › Mockups) and taps Send for approval. The customer gets it with two buttons: ✅ Confirm mockup and ✏️ Need changes.

    ✏️ Need changes → the lead goes back to Need mockup and the bot asks what to change. The designer updates it and sends again.

  5. 5

    The customer confirms — the order is created

    CRM, automatically

    On ✅ Confirm the CRM raises the order from the lead (each product, colour, quantity, the catalogue price for that quantity, ₹800 digitising for embroidery, 5% GST) and sends the customer the order summary with the 50% advance payment link.

    If a quantity is a range or a price is “on request”, nothing is guessed: the customer is thanked and the chat waits for you, with a note saying what’s missing. Create the order from the chat.

  6. 6

    Advance paid → production

    CRMYou

    The Razorpay payment updates the order and the lead to Partially paid. Confirm sizes with the customer if they’re not yet on the order.

  7. 7

    Balance and dispatch

    You

    When production is done, send the balance link from the order messages in the chat. Once fully paid (Paid), the order is dispatched and the tracking goes to the customer.

Where to look in the inbox

Four lists at the top left. Work them left to right.

Reply nowfirst

Customers waiting for a reply — your chats and new ones nobody has taken. The red number on a chat is how many messages are waiting. Keep this list at zero.

My chatssecond

Every open chat assigned to you. Use it for follow-ups and for checking where each of your leads stands.

New leadswhen Reply now is clear

Chats nobody has taken yet. Open one and reply — it becomes yours.

All chatsreference

Everything open, whoever has it. For managers, or to find a customer.

The status picker under the lists filters any of them — choose Important to see the big orders, or Follow-up for the ones to chase. The date filter at the top narrows everything to a period.

Which chat first

  1. Reply now, with a red number. A customer is waiting. Reply within 5 minutes during working hours.
  2. Important (more than 50 pieces). Reply and call. These are the biggest orders.
  3. Mock completed with no answer from the customer. Nudge them to tap Confirm, or ask what to change.
  4. Follow-up. Chase quotes and payments promised for today.
  5. Reply windows running out. A chat showing 2h 10m to reply needs a message before it closes.
  6. New leads. Take the newest first — they’re still on their phones.

Your 9-hour shift

First 30 minutes

Clear the night

  • Reply now: answer everyone who wrote overnight.
  • Important: call the big ones.
  • Follow-up: list who to chase today.
Every hour

Back to zero

  • Reply now back to zero.
  • Check timers under 3 hours.
  • Return missed calls.
In between

Move leads forward

  • Fill in quantities, colours and billing on the Lead tab.
  • Send samples and quotes.
  • Chase confirmations and advances.
Last 30 minutes

Hand over cleanly

  • Nobody left in Reply now.
  • Set statuses right (Follow-up for anything pending).
  • Write a note on any chat the next person needs to know about.

WhatsApp’s 24-hour reply window

WhatsApp lets you type freely for 24 hours after the customer’s last message. After that you can only send an approved template until they write again. The timer at the top of every chat shows where you are.

Five rules

  • Reply now stays at zero.A red number is a customer waiting.
  • Reply within 5 minutes.Most leads choose whoever answers first.
  • Exact numbers on the Lead tab.“50 to 200” can’t become an order; “120 pcs” can.
  • Never promise a delivery date unless production has confirmed it.
  • Leave a note on any chat the next person needs to understand.
