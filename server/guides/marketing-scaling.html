The playbook the marketing team follows for every product: work out its numbers, test it, judge it on ROAS, scale the winners and stop the rest.

Step 3

Campaigns by role

Each product gets a role, and the role sets its campaigns. A product never shares a budget with one that has a different break-even. In every testing campaign one creative is one ad set with a ₹1,000 budget.

Hero

The proven winner, or the product with the most revenue

  • One testing and one scaling campaign.
  • Judged at ₹3,000–4,000 spend per creative.
  • Scale 20–30% every 48–72 h while ROAS holds; step back if it stays under the winner line for 2 days.
Supporting

Every other product

  • One testing campaign per product.
  • A scaling campaign only after a win.
  • Same rule as the hero.
Retargeting

All products, shared

  • One campaign for visitors, carts and past buyers.
  • Judged on weekly ROAS: keep it at the winner line or better.
  • Cross-sell a related product, never the one just bought.

A supporting product becomes a hero once it holds ROAS at or above its winner line at a budget worth scaling — it then gets its own scaling campaign.

Lift order value with bundles

A product that keeps little per order can’t carry ad cost alone. Raising the value of the first order is cheaper than finding more buyers.

  • Bundle products that go together, so one ad click sells a bigger order.
  • Offer a multi-piece price on low-priced products.
  • Set a free-shipping threshold above today’s average order value, then test it.
  • Show the bundle on the product page and in the cart, not only in the ad.

Set every bundle price and threshold from the Step 1 numbers, so an offer never drops below break-even.

Creative split

Creative effort follows the roles. Of every ten new ads, about six go to the hero, three to supporting products and one to an experiment — a new format or audience. A starting rule, adjusted with results.

Adding a new product

  1. Get its price and costs, work out the Step 1 numbers and add a register row.
  2. Create one testing campaign with a ₹1,000 ad set for each creative.
  3. Let each creative spend ₹3,000–4,000, then apply the rule: scale, run on once, or stop.
  4. Record the result and the creative angle in the register.
  5. Move a winner into its own scaling campaign and add the product to the shared retargeting campaign.
